# 🌾 KisanSetu

**KisanSetu** is a smart digital platform designed to empower farmers by providing easy access to essential agricultural information, market prices, weather updates, government schemes, and useful farming resources — all in one place.

The platform aims to bridge the gap between farmers and reliable digital information by presenting important agricultural data in a **simple, accessible, and user-friendly interface**. KisanSetu helps farmers make better-informed decisions regarding crop cultivation, market selling, weather conditions, and available government support.

## 🚜 Key Features

* 📊 **Live Mandi Prices** – Access updated market prices to help farmers understand current crop rates.
* 🌦️ **Weather Updates** – Provides weather-related information useful for planning agricultural activities.
* 🌾 **Crop & Farming Information** – Useful resources and information to support better farming decisions.
* 🏛️ **Government Schemes** – Helps farmers discover relevant government schemes and agricultural benefits.
* 🔊 **Hindi Voice Assistance** – Makes the platform more accessible by providing information in a farmer-friendly voice interface.
* 🌓 **Light & Dark Mode** – User-friendly interface with theme customization.
* 📱 **Responsive Design** – Designed to work across desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.
* 🔄 **Dynamic Data** – Designed to integrate real-time agricultural data and APIs for continuously updated information.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Farmers often face difficulties accessing reliable and timely information about **market prices, weather conditions, government schemes, and agricultural resources**. Information may be scattered across different platforms, making it difficult to access and understand.

**KisanSetu addresses this problem by bringing important agricultural services and information together on a single digital platform.**

## 💡 Our Vision

Our vision is to create a **digital bridge between farmers and technology**, making agricultural information more accessible, understandable, and useful.

KisanSetu aims to contribute towards a smarter and more digitally connected agricultural ecosystem where farmers can access the information they need to make informed decisions.

## 🛠️ Technology

The project is built using modern web technologies and can be extended with APIs and backend services for real-time agricultural data.

**Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript
**Data & APIs:** Weather and market-data integrations
**Interface:** Responsive Web Design
**Accessibility:** Hindi Voice Assistance
